Rajendra Mittal

November 14, 1947 – 2021
Baburam, my grandfather
"Although he was unable to complete his college education, Mittal understood the transformative power of learning and ensured that his children and grandchildren received the best education possible."
Life & Journey

A Life Lived Larger Than Life

🌱

Indore Origins & Familial Love

Born on November 14th, 1947, in the vibrant city of Indore, India, Rajendra Mittal was the youngest of five brothers and the cherished favorite of five sisters. He embodied the true spirit of familial connection, warmth, and generosity.

🎓

Devotion to Education & Music

With unwavering commitment, Mittal encouraged his children and grandchildren to excel academically and creatively. Rahul Mittal remembers his grandfather as the first to ask about school performance and a constant supporter of Rahul's music and extracurricular pursuits. Every single morning, Mittal personally dropped Rahul and his sister off at the school bus stop.

🧪

Pioneer in Food Technology

Driven by a passion for science and food technology, Mittal revolutionized the traditional Indian pickle industry with innovative recipes for condiments and ketchups, leaving a lasting flavorful imprint on the culinary landscape.

🕊️

Family & Loving Legacy

Survived by his loving wife Suman, children Sandeep and Shilpa, and grandchildren Rahul and Anshika. Though he passed away in 2021 due to COVID-19, his spirit and generosity live on through the achievements and aspirations of his descendants.

Enduring Vision

The Rajendra Mittal International Excellence Award at FIU

In honor of Rajendra Mittal's dream of "education for all" and comprehensive personal development, the Mittal family established the Rajendra Mittal International Excellence Award at Florida International University (FIU).

This scholarship is dedicated to First-Time-In-College (FTIC) international students pursuing STEM fields, with preference for students enrolled in the Honors College and the College of Engineering and Computing. The award recognizes students who exemplify academic excellence, leadership, and well-rounded involvement in extracurricular activities both at FIU and in the broader community.

Florida International University FTIC International Students STEM & Honors College College of Engineering & Computing

In his honor, the Mittal family also supports the Round Rock Public Library in Texas, empowering patrons with high-quality educational and community resources.
